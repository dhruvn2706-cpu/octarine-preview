About the studio

We Help Projects Be Understood.

We are a 3D visualization studio specialising in high-quality architectural and interior renders — helping architects, interior designers and developers present their projects clearly before execution.

What we do

From concept to final render, every detail is carefully crafted to achieve high levels of realism and visual clarity. We focus on materials, lighting and composition to create accurate and compelling 3D visualizations that help architects, designers and developers present their projects with confidence.

Why it matters

A drawing asks someone to imagine a building. A render lets them stand in it. That difference is what closes an approval, wins a competition, or convinces a buyer to commit money years before handover — which is why accuracy matters as much as beauty. We model from your drawings rather than approximating, so what you show is what gets built.

How we work

Everything happens inside one studio — modelling, lighting, art direction and post. Nothing is handed off mid-project, so the frame you approve at the clay stage is the frame you get at the end, only finished.

Where we work

From Mumbai, India, on projects across India and internationally — Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Azerbaijan, Indonesia, Sweden and Finland among them. Residential, commercial, hospitality and mixed-use.
