Launch Events

One evening that has to do the work of a month

8 Apr 2026·5 min read

Everything else the project produces is looked at — on a screen, on paper, from a car. The launch event is the only moment where people are physically inside the identity. That is a rare and expensive opportunity, and it is frequently spent on a stage, a screen and a buffet.

Decide what the evening is for

An event that tries to serve buyers, channel partners, press and internal stakeholders at once serves none of them. Pick the primary audience and design for them:

  • Channel partner launch. The goal is enthusiasm and clarity. Heavy on product detail, commission structure and comparison. Partners should leave able to pitch it tomorrow.
  • Buyer preview. The goal is desire and urgency. Light on detail, heavy on experience — the show unit, the model, the view, the neighbourhood.
  • Press and civic. The goal is credibility and a story worth writing. Different room, different evening.

The environment is the collateral

Applied properly, the identity turns a venue into the project: entry, wayfinding, the model table, the render wall, the show unit walkthrough, the photo moment, the takeaway. Every one of those is a designed surface, and together they are more persuasive than any presentation from a stage.

Especially the physical model and the render wall. People will spend twenty unhurried minutes at a good model — more sustained attention than any other asset in the launch receives.

Design the takeaway before the evening

What does a guest leave with, physically and digitally? A well-made leave-behind, and a follow-up message that reaches them the same night while the room is still fresh. An event with no designed exit is an event that ends when people walk out of the door.

Photograph it properly

The event produces content for the next three months — social, partner updates, the press release, the credibility slide in every future deck. Brief a photographer against a shot list, not vibes. The images you actually need are the model with people around it, the show unit in use, and the room full. Not the ribbon cutting.

And afterwards

The measure is not attendance. It is bookings in the following fortnight and how many partners started leading with your project. If the evening was designed around one audience and one argument, both numbers move.
